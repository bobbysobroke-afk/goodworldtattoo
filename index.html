Welcome to Good World

Good World Tattoo is a relaxed, artist-driven tattoo studio in Sacramento. Getting tattooed should feel exciting, comfortable and personal — not intimidating.

Before & After

Getting Ready

  • Eat a real meal before you come in, and bring a snack and water.
  • Sleep well the night before. Rested and sober makes for a better session.
  • Wear comfortable clothes that give easy access to the area.
  • Bring headphones, a book or a friend if that helps you settle.
  • Skip sunburn and heavy lotion on the area that day.

Deposit, ID and age requirements: to be confirmed by the shop.

Aftercare

Your artist sends you home with instructions for your specific tattoo — follow those first. General good practice: keep it clean, moisturize lightly, stay out of the sun, and don't pick.
